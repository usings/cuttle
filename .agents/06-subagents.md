# Subagents

Use when delegation is permitted and independent judgment or parallel work justifies its cost.

Give each agent a bounded objective, context, constraints, applicable guidance, and expected evidence. Allow inspection of related paths to challenge assumptions; do not assume the agent inherits loaded guidance.

Use disjoint files or separate worktrees for concurrent edits. Agree on shared interfaces before dispatch; request changes, checks, findings, and unresolved assumptions on return.

The coordinator owns repairs, integration, and verification of the combined result, including interactions between agents' changes.
